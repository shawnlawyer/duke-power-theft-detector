## Free customer download

1. The customer signs in to their own UI online account and opens Energy Analyzer. New accounts may need 72 hours before data appears.
2. The customer downloads their consumption data as XML or CSV and chooses to provide that file to Home Energy Watch. A PDF is not an interval-data upload.
3. Check the file's actual timestamps, interval lengths, units, account identifiers, time zone, gaps, and estimated-reading indicators before claiming compatibility. Daily totals alone cannot establish an overnight baseline.

This route does not require a provider registration to obtain the customer's own download. It does not establish an automated connection, vendor certification, or verified Home Energy Watch support.

## Monthly historical usage release

UI's [historical-usage instructions](https://www.uinet.com/suppliers_and_partners/electric_suppliers_aggregators/for_suppliers_aggregators/historical_usage_requests) require possession of the original signed release before disclosure. Send it only after authorization to Supplier Relations AD-2A, The United Illuminating Company, 180 Marsh Hill Road, Orange, CT 06477. An advance email to supplier.relations@uinet.com can start preparation, but does not replace the original.

The [consultant release](https://www.uinet.com/documents/1678076/1704127/UI_Third_party_HU_release_form.pdf/bf05179a-26f3-7508-e624-e597c025bfd5?t=1643356035366&version=1.0) requires the consultant's name, address, contact and phone; the customer's account name, 13-digit POD ID or attached account list, phone, email, printed name/title, signature and date; and an expiration selection. Its scope is monthly historical usage, not an interval feed.

## Paid interval service application

Start at [Customer Data Requests](https://www.uinet.com/suppliers_and_partners/electric_suppliers_aggregators/for_suppliers_aggregators/interval_data_requests). Follow the documents by title and contents: the page's two link labels currently lead to the opposite document types.

Under the [March 2025 instructions](https://www.uinet.com/documents/1678076/1704122/UI%2BCustomer%2BDate%2BRequest%2B03-10-2025.pdf/d941774d-1b69-11d4-e83c-fcc37b699c82?t=1741791549461), an unlicensed consultant needs both the UI consultant release and the customer's signature on the service agreement. The PURA licensed-supplier alternative is not a general analytics-vendor shortcut. Print and complete the agreement by hand, scan it, and send the authorized request to energytracer@uinet.com.

The agreement requires a service selection; data type and dates for custom requests; customer contact/address and 14-digit account number(s); customer authorization; requestor business/contact details; and billing details with a signature agreeing to charges. Do not confuse the 14-digit account number with the release form's 13-digit POD ID. UI states seven business days for subscription setup and thirty for one-time requests after the signed contract and required release arrive. The recurring option delivers 15-minute readings daily and requires 30 days' written termination notice.
