Careful review rules

Three rules stay visible before anyone acts.

Home Energy Watch keeps the review grounded by showing where the record is strong, where it is limited, and what still needs human context.

01

Source context stays attached.

File type, upload time, account, meter, coverage period, and refresh source remain visible in the record.

02

Gaps are shown honestly.

Missing intervals, estimated readings, daylight-saving shifts, and partial windows are shown before interpretation.

03

Notes do not become findings.

Human context stays separate from measured patterns until the reviewer saves a supported finding.
