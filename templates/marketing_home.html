{# ================= FAQ ================= #}
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where does my energy data come from?

From your electric utility. Most utilities let you download your usage history as a Green Button file or spreadsheet, and Home Energy Watch reads interval XML and CSV files directly. Step-by-step download guides are included for many utilities.

Which utilities can connect automatically?

Automatic updates are available where a utility has approved Home Energy Watch and the connection is switched on for your account. Everyone else can upload their utility's download at any time and get the same analysis.

What does a flagged night mean?

It means overnight use rose well above your usual baseline, a good moment to take a closer look. Each flag shows the rule that produced it.

Can I share my reports?

Yes. Download reports as files, or give someone you trust access to your account as a viewer or manager.
