How it works

From energy data to everyday clarity.

Home Energy Watch organizes your electricity information into meaningful patterns, easy-to-read reports and useful insights.

Step 1

Add your home and utility account

Create your account, name your home and enter the electric account you want to follow. Tell us about your heating, cooling and major appliances whenever you like.

  • Sign in with an emailed link or a passkey, with no password to remember
  • Invite family members or advisers as viewers or managers
  • Give permission for your utility data in plain language, and withdraw it at any time
Step 2

Connect or import your electricity readings

Upload the usage file your utility provides: Green Button XML, interval XML or CSV. Where your utility supports it, approve an automatic connection once and new readings arrive on their own.

Each new file adds to one continuous history. Earlier readings stay in place.

  • Download guides for many utilities, written step by step
  • Readings kept at the resolution your utility records
  • Format detected for you, with clear messages if a file can't be read
Step 3

Explore trends, insights and reports

See your overnight baseline, the nights that break from it, any day in detail and how one period compares with another. Download reports whenever you need them.

Questions about getting started

How long does setup take?

Most people are looking at their first analysis within a few minutes of downloading their utility file.

Do I need special equipment?

No. Home Energy Watch uses the readings your electric meter already sends to your utility.

Can I add more history later?

Yes. Upload newer or older files at any time and they join the same timeline.
