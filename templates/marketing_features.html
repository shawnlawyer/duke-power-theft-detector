Overnight baseline and anomaly detection

The quiet hours are where a home should be most predictable. Home Energy Watch measures that baseline every night and flags the nights that break from it.

  • Overnight window and thresholds you can adjust
  • Each flag names the rule that produced it
  • Sharp jumps pinpointed to the interval where they began

Appliance and weather insights

Put the readings in context. Record the equipment in your home, then compare what it can draw with what the meter recorded. Flagged days include the weather that day.

  • An equipment inventory for heating, cooling, water heating and more
  • High, low, feels-like, rain and wind for flagged days
  • A history of equipment changes alongside your usage
