About Home Energy Watch

The pattern behind the electric bill.

Home Energy Watch turns the electricity readings a utility already provides into a clear, organized record of how a home uses energy. Homeowners see their overnight baseline, compare periods and review the days that changed. Professionals and utility commissions get the same record with the rule behind every finding.

A flagged pattern is a reason to look more closely. What it means is for the account holder to decide.
